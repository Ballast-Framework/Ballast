# Code of Conduct

Ballast adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of conduct. The full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies to everyone who participates in the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space, and when representing the project in public.
- Critique controls, mappings and ideas, not people. Disagreement about a control is welcome; harassment and personal attacks are not.
- Disclose relevant vendor affiliations when you propose changes, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#governance), and do not use project spaces to market products.

This summary does not replace the Contributor Covenant; where they differ, the Covenant applies.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by private LinkedIn message to [Raoul Hira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/raoulhira) or [Avishkar (Avi) Nikum](https://www.linkedin.com/in/avinikum). Reports are reviewed as promptly as possible and handled confidentially. If a report concerns one maintainer, contact the other; the maintainer concerned will not handle the report.
