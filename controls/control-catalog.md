| <a id="bal-3-1"></a>[BAL-3.1](#bal-3-1) | [Autonomy level](../guides/tiering.md#autonomy-levels) enforcement and promotion | Each agent's initiation mode and its [autonomy level](../guides/tiering.md#autonomy-levels) ([A0](../guides/tiering.md#a0)-[A4](../guides/tiering.md#a4)) for each action class are recorded, and levels are enforced by permissions, approval settings and tool policy outside the model. | <a id="bal-3-6"></a>[BAL-3.6](#bal-3-6) | Human approval of high-risk actions | A person authorized for the action class approves each action in these classes: payments and financial commitments; data transfer outside the trust boundary or to recipients not already entitled; secret retrieval or privileged credential use; external communications; destructive or irreversible operations, which in production also require a verified restore point; production changes, including protected-branch merges, releases and deployments; permission, access, credential or account-recovery changes; changes to security controls or monitoring, including closing or suppressing alerts; installing unapproved dependencies, extensions or MCP servers; and publishing to shared, public or personal destinations.
