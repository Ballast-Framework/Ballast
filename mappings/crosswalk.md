# Standards Crosswalk

This crosswalk shows where Ballast controls mitigate risks named in AI threat taxonomies and contribute to outcomes in AI risk-management and management-system frameworks. The per-domain tables at the end of this page are generated from [`control-mappings.csv`](control-mappings.csv); correct a mapping by changing the CSV, not the tables.

## How to read it

- **Indicative, not certifying.** A mapping shows that a control contributes to a referenced outcome or mitigates a referenced risk. It is not a claim of equivalence. A mapping does not establish conformance with the referenced framework, or compliance with any law or regulation.
- **Control-level and generated.** Each row of `control-mappings.csv` links one Ballast control to one reference, with its framework version. The tables below are generated from it. In v1.0 the rows are unqualified; per-row relationship qualifiers and rationales, defined below, are planned for v1.1 (see [Known gaps](../KNOWN-GAPS.md)).
- **Relationship qualifiers for threat taxonomies (planned for v1.1)** ([OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/GenAI-LLM-Top10/blob/main/2026/README.md), [OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/), [MITRE ATLAS](https://github.com/mitre-atlas/atlas-data/releases/tag/v2026.09) techniques):
  - **primary**: the control is one of the main mitigations for the risk or technique.
  - **contributing**: the control reduces likelihood or impact, or supports detection or response, but would not address the risk on its own.
- **Outcome and management-system frameworks** ([NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) subcategories, [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) Annex A): a mapping means the control produces evidence toward that outcome. Many of these outcomes are broader than security, so a mapping does not mean the control covers the whole outcome.
- **Only implemented references are listed.** A reference appears only where at least one Ballast control implements or contributes to it. If a reference is absent, Ballast does not claim to address it.
- **Check [Control Tier](../guides/tiering.md#control-tiers) and applicability.** A reference may be covered only by a [T2](../guides/tiering.md#t2) or [T3](../guides/tiering.md#t3) control, or by a control whose capability tags do not apply to a given asset. Check each control's [Control Tier](../guides/tiering.md#control-tiers), scope and applies_to value before claiming coverage for a specific asset (see the [tiering guide](../guides/tiering.md)).
- **No standard text is reproduced.** Only identifiers and short paraphrased labels are used; no requirement text from any framework appears here. Consult the source publications for the full requirements.
- **Cite the version with the ID.** Identifiers change between editions and releases, so cite each reference with the framework version listed below.
